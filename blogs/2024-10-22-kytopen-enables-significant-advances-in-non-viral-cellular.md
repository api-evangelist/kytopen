Successful engineering of macrophages is of critical importance for next-generation immunotherapies, including solid tumors. Kytopen optimization study using the Flowfect Discover™ 96-well platform demonstrated >90% gene delivery efficiency and viability of monocyte-derived macrophages. A single, optimized protocol was transferred to commercial scale on the Flowfect Tx™ GMP platform in less than three weeks.
