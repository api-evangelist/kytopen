CAMBRIDGE, Mass. and SAN FRANCISCO, Calif., – April 07, 2026 – Kytopen Corp., a leader in non-viral, continuous flow cellular engineering, today announced a Series B financing led by Telegraph Hill Partners, with participation from existing investors. The funding will accelerate Kytopen’s global commercial expansion and drive further adoption of its Flowfect® technology platform across the U.S.
