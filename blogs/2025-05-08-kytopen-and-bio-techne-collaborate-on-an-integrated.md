Kytopen and Bio-Techne announce partnership on an integrated cellular engineering workflow to expedite cell therapy manufacturing. The TcBuster™ system offers a fast and scalable method to integrate large, multigene cargos with high editing efficiency in a single vector, while Flowfect ® continuous flow technology enables the gentle, high-efficiency processing of billions of cells in minutes. The companies’ combined technologies deliver high gene insertion efficiencies and high cell viabilities, enabling a non-viral gene-editing workflow ideal for the development of advanced cell therapies.
