Kytopen and BlueWhale Bio join forces to develop non-viral cell therapy manufacturing workflow. BlueWhale Bio’s Synecta™ cell-derived nanoparticles (CDNPs) technology mimics the immune system’s natural T-cell priming ex vivo and are built to scale, while Kytopen’s Flowfect® continuous flow technology enables the gentle, high-efficiency processing of billions of cells in just minutes. This partnership has the potential to change how cell therapies are manufactured, giving patients access to more effective, lifesaving, personalized treatments more quickly.
