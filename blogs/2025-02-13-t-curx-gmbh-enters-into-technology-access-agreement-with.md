Kytopen announces expansion of its partnership program into Europe with its agreement with T-CURX GmbH, a biotech company based in Würzburg, Germany. T-CURX, a clinically focused therapeutic company developing personalized immunotherapies, will have on-site access to Kytopen’s Flowfect Tx™ cellular engineering platform. A critical deciding factor for T-CURX, was the Flowfect Tx™ platform proof of validation for GMP manufacturing of engineered cells at clinical and commercial scale.
