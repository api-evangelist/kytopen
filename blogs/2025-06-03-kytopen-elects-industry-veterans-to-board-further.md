Kytopen appoints new board of directors as part of its commercial expansion of the Flowfect Tx ® GMP cellular engineering platform. Industry veteran Henry Hebel joins Kytopen’s board bringing operational expertise from Aldevron. Joe Gentile, recognized life sciences leader and former board observer, elected Chairman of the Board.
